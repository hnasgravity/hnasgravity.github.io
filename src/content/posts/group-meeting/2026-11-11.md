## Invited Talk

Mr. Dhiraj Kuniyal (Department of Physics & Astrophysics, University of Delhi, India) visited the Institute for Gravitational Wave Astronomy, Henan Academy of Sciences, from 11 to 14 November 2026. As part of his academic visit, he gave a talk to our group.

- **Title:** *Cosmological Bouncing Scenarios in Modified Gravity*

## Meeting Minutes

Mr. Dhiraj Kuniyal presented his work on cosmological bouncing scenarios in modified gravity, which offer an alternative to the initial singularity of standard cosmology. The talk was followed by a lively discussion with group members on the theoretical framework and its possible observational implications.
